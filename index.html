Why I Chose Software Development: My Journey of Building Solutions

Software development is more than writing lines of code. It is about solving real-world problems, creating meaningful experiences, and continuously learning.

My journey into software development began with a curiosity about how websites and applications work behind the scenes. That curiosity eventually led me to study Computer Technology at Maseno University, where I developed a strong foundation in programming, databases, networking, and software engineering.

As I continued learning, I gained experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and PHP. These technologies allowed me to build responsive websites and interactive web applications that focus on usability, performance, and clean design.

One of the projects that challenged me the most was Chips Point. Developing the frontend was exciting, but connecting it to the backend required patience and persistence. I spent many hours debugging API requests, fixing routing issues, and ensuring that data flowed correctly between the client and the server.

I believe every challenge in programming is an opportunity to learn something new. Whether it is deploying an application, improving website security, optimizing performance, or creating a better user experience, there is always room for growth.

Technology continues to evolve rapidly, and I enjoy exploring modern tools, frameworks, and best practices that help me become a better developer. My goal is not only to write code but also to create software that makes people's lives easier and businesses more efficient.

Through this blog, I will share tutorials, development tips, project experiences, lessons learned, and insights into the technologies I use. Whether you are a beginner learning to code or someone interested in web development, I hope my experiences inspire and help you on your own journey.

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. I invite you to explore my projects, follow my progress, and join me as I continue learning and building innovative solutions.

The journey of a developer never truly ends—it simply evolves with every project, every challenge, and every line of code.
